## 📜 Official Rules

1. **It Must Still Be Possible to Cancel**
   - **Hostile, NOT broken.**
   - A determined, clever user *must* have a functional, valid path to complete cancellation and reach an end/success state. No hard infinite dead-ends or unclickable dummy elements as the sole path.
2. **Fake Everything**
   - No real accounts, payment gateways, or personal user data.
   - Use a completely fictional service / subscription product (e.g., *MegaStream*, *CloudHyperSync*, *OmegaSaaS*, *CatFacts Pro*).
3. **Keep It Friendly (PG-Rated Evil)**
   - The UX and copy should be delightfully evil, comedic, and satirical, but **never offensive, hateful, or abusive**. Keep it PG / workplace-safe.
4. **Any Stack, AI Welcome**
   - Plain HTML/CSS/JS, React, Vue, Svelte, Next.js, or any tooling. AI assistance is fully permitted and encouraged.

## 🎭 The Dark Pattern Playbook (Inspiration)

- **Confuse Them (`?!`):**
  - Inverted CTA hierarchy (bright green button renews; tiny low-contrast button advances).
  - Double and triple negatives (*"Don't not uncheck this box to keep your subscription active"*).
  - Branching multi-step mazes that silently loop back to Step 1 upon a single mistake.
- **Guilt-Trip / Confirmshaming (`:(`):**
  - Emotional blackmail (*"Click here to confirm you hate puppies and prefer your files deleted forever"*).
  - Fake countdown timers that accelerate or reset.
  - Catastrophic loss lists (*"Cancelling forfeits 47 unused benefits, 9,200 points, and your profile picture"*).
- **Hide the Exit (`->|`):**
  - Moving/evasive buttons on mouse hover.
  - Camouflaged links (e.g., light grey on white background, 8px font).
  - Absurd requirements (e.g., hold button down for exactly 7.3 seconds, solve an unhinged slider puzzle).
