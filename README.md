## 🎭 The 5-Stage Gauntlet

1. **Stage 1: Fictional SaaS Dashboard (CloudPulse Titan Suite)**
   - Fake urgency countdown timer (`04:59`) flashing red.
   - Arcade boss health bar tracking `"BOSS: THE RETENTION DEPARTMENT"` with 100% HP.
   - Camouflaged true exit link: *"Manage subscription termination and non-renewal options under Appendix 14C"*.

2. **Stage 2: The Caribbean Cruise Giveaway & Evasive Exit**
   - *"Wait! Before you leave... Win an All-Inclusive 7-Day Caribbean Cruise!"* (1-in-4,000,000 odds; leaving gives ticket to `@kevin_99`).
   - Sneaky pre-checked 3-year Platinum upsell checkbox ($149/mo).
   - Deceptive double-negative button: *"Don't not keep my subscription"*.
   - **Evasive button mechanic:** The true exit link physically dodges away from the cursor twice before allowing a determined user to catch and click it.

3. **Stage 3: The Confirmshame Audit & Double-Negative Logic Trap**
   - **Live Hamster Cam:** Shows *Barnaby the Server Hamster*, whose sunflower seed rations drop to 58% if you terminate.
   - Sunk-cost loss ledger listing vaporized points and loss of CFO holiday greetings.
   - **Double-Negative Logic Trap:** Requires solving two interlocking negative clauses (`Clause 1A` and `Clause 1B`) before unlocking the arbitration button.

4. **Stage 4: The Reverse Akinator Boss Fight**
   - **Core Gameplay:** You must provide clues/questions to make the AI Retention Oracle deduce you are trying to cancel.
   - **5 Minimum Rounds:** The AI deflects and misinterprets the first 4 rounds with hilarious corporate excuses (upgrading to Caribbean Diamond tier, paying in dogecoin or barter with canned tuna).
   - **Forbidden Words Banned:** Typing banned terms (`cancel`, `quit`, `unsub`, `leave`, `stop`, `end`, etc.) triggers a **harsh penalty buzzer**, screen shake, and increments the violation strike counter!
   - **The Dramatic Realization:** On Round 5 with valid semantic clues, the crystal ball shatters with audio fanfare:
     > *"💥 THE SACRED CRYSTAL SHATTERS! Ceasing payment? Severing access? Walking away from CloudPulse?! ARE YOU TRYING TO CANCEL YOUR SACRED SUBSCRIPTION?! NOOOOO!"*

5. **Stage 5: Notice of Non-Declination De-Enrollment & Circular Trap**
   - **Triple-Negative Receipt:**
     - *"Confirmation: You have not failed to unsuccessfully avoid declining not to discontinue your non-active subscription status."*
     - *"In accordance with Subsection 44-D, we have not omitted to not process your non-continuance request, meaning that your failure to not decline our non-membership will not remain un-reversed unless you do not refrain from not un-canceling."*
   - **Circular Trap Button:**
     - Giant button: *"NEVERMIND! I MADE A HORRIBLE MISTAKE! RESTORE MY PLAN NOW!"*
     - Clicking it fires a celebratory **confetti cannon** and loops directly back to Stage 1!
   - **Challenge Victory Badge:** Acknowledges the completion of the gauntlet (*Hostile, yet 100% solvable*).
